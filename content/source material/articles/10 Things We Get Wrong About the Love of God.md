Summary and thoughts of this article: [10 Things We Get Wrong About the Love of God | Crossway Articles](https://www.crossway.org/articles/10-things-we-get-wrong-about-the-love-of-god)

1. **He doesn't owe us His love**. He's sovereign, he can freely give and freely withhold His love to anyone. Seems that there are two types of love, one of which is [[Saving love|saving or electing love]], which it doesn't give to “hell-deserving sinners”.
2. **His love is not monolithic**. His love can vary depending on His purpose, good will, among other reasons. Like in the way He displays his kindness, mercy, and patience to everyone (common grace) versus how he redeems, regenerates and gives eternal life to those who trust in Him (special grace). According to D. A. Carson, there are five ways the bible speaks of God's love:
	1. Love of the Father to the Son.
	2. Love for His creation (everything He made was “good”).
	3. Love for the fallen world.
	4. Love for the people He elects (Israel, the church, individuals)
	5. Conditional love (for those who stay in Him, keep His commandments, fear Him).
3. **His love doesn't mean that everyone will be saved**. That is only reserved for the elect. If someone goes to hell, it was not an act of unfairness but in accord to perfect justice.
4. **The death of Christ doesn't secure God's love**. Jesus died because God loves us, not the opposite. If so, it would mean that Christ had to die so we could get a small dose of love from an unwilling, reluctant God. [John 3:16](https://www.esv.org/verses/John%203%3A16/)
5. **He doesn't love because He knew we would love Him**. There's nothing we can be or choose to do we can earn His love. In fact, everything He did is because He loved us first. [Rom. 8:29](https://www.esv.org/verses/Rom.%208%3A29/)
6. **He doesn't love us because mankind fell**. His love is eternal. “Before the foundations of the world, He selected us” ([Ephesians 1:4-5](https://www.esv.org/verses/Eph.%201%3A4%E2%80%935/)).
7. **His love will never be revoked or overturned**. “Who shall separate us from the love of Christ? Shall tribulation, or distress, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or danger, or sword? Trouble or hardship or persecution or famine or nakedness or danger or sword?” ([Rom. 8:35](https://www.esv.org/verses/Rom.%208%3A35/))
8. **His love doesn't mean He will always save us from suffering or that He will never discipline us**. Bad time, so far from being a sign that God rejects us, it's evidence of His fatherly love for us. “My son, do not regard lightly the discipline of the Lord, nor be weary when reproved by him. For the Lord disciplines the one he loves, and chastises every son he receives” ([Heb. 12:5–6](https://www.esv.org/verses/Heb.%2012%3A5%E2%80%936/)).
9. God's love is not just to save sinners. It also leads us to mutual love, obedience, and security in Christ. ([1 John 4:12](https://www.esv.org/verses/1%20John%204%3A12/), [1 John 4:7–11](https://www.esv.org/verses/1%20John%204%3A7%E2%80%9311/))
10. **God's love for himself is not selfish**. God's primary goal is to secure praise, honor, and glory for himself. But that doesn't mean that He doesn't love us. All enjoyment lead to praise and adoration of something, therefore what is better that to enjoy the most excellent being in the universe, whose pleasure is in us loving Him! So, for God to seek his glory in your worship of him (that’s his love for himself) is the most loving thing he can do for you.
