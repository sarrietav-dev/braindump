# James 1
## James 1:2-18

We ought to have joy in trials because they enable us to have steadfastness, which allows God to perfect us.

We have to ask God for wisdom in faith, because that who doubts is like “a wave tossed by the wind, unstable in all his ways”.

We are like the flower of the grass, which bloom in all his splendor, but in a moment it withers away and it's beauty perishes.

And those who stay faithful under trials will be blessed, and they will be given the crown of life

## James 1:19-27

It's not enough to hear the word of God, we have to obey it! We might be too quick to share our opinions and not let His word pierce through our head to our heart.

# James 2

## James 2:1-6

The passage seem to address some person that gives preference to the rich (maybe to have more influence and power), and sets aside the poor. Which is against the nature of impartiality of God.

## James 2:7-13

James says that if you show partiality, you commit the sin against the commandment of “You shall love your neighbor as yourself”, and to support that he says that if someone fails to keep one law, he becomes guilty of all of it.

## James 2:14-26

In this difficult passage, James seems to contradict Paul on the claim that we are not justified by works. But in reality, he is saying that works are the evidence of your faith. True faith *produces* works. He also gives examples of the works of Abraham when he was about to kill his son, on Rehab when she trusted God and allowed the Hebrew spies to escape Jericho.

According to the ESV Study Bible, when James uses the word justify, what he means is that *works demonstrate that someone has been justified*.
