# Colossians 1

> [!tip]- Jesus is the main thing
> Jesus is the main thing. Nothing is greater than Him. 
> 
> He's the image of God. 
> 
> Everything was made through Him. 
> 
> Nothing is made without Him. 
> 
> Nothing lacks in Him.
> 
> Bow to the Maker!

# Colossians 2

> [!tip]- Everything I do is because He did it first
> Everything I do is because He did it first to me.
> 
> I'm owned by Him, He is my Boss, my Master, my Lord. He acts, we respond.  
> 
> We receive Him because He came to us. We thank Him because He first does much for us. We walk in Him because He fills us.

# Colossians 3

>[!tip]- Let the word of God dwell in you
>Let the word of God dwell in you. 
>
>It strengthens a man's heart to not go off the rails.
>
>It enables us to put our mind on eternity, to put to death earthy desires, to not lie, to be compassionate, forgiving, loving, thankful, with inner peace.
