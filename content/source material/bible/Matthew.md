# Matthew 5

## Matthew 5:3-12
Speaks of the joy that those who are going through bad times should feel.

## Matthew 5:13
Do we preserve the earth? What is salt in the Bible? How can we become tasteless?

## Matthew 5:14-16
What is light? We should not hide our faith, but be an example to others and thus glorify God.

## Matthew 5:17-18
God's word is constant, never changes, everything is fulfilled.

## Matthew 5:19-20
God rejoices in the one who fulfills and teaches his word.  
What is the righteousness of the Pharisees? #todo

## Matthew 5:21-22
Anger is considered sin to God.

## Matthew 5:23-26
We must seek reconciliation before communicating with God.

## Matthew 5:27-28
Lust is also sin in God's eyes.  

It's not only the actions, but the state of your heart.

## Matthew 5:29-30
We must make drastic decisions when it comes to overcoming sin.  
What decisions should I make? #todo

## Matthew 5:31-32
Divorce for mere displeasure is not good to God because it goes against the nature of his divine representation.

## Matthew 5:33-37
Don't make oaths. Why can't we make them?  
What does it mean to swear by something?  
What implications does it have for that something?  
Why does responding more than a yes or no come from evil? #todo

## Matthew 5:38-42
Respond to evil with good.  
Always go one step further based on love.  
Don't go two kilometers more because you want to make the other person suffer, but because you really want to do good to them.  

What is not resisting the one who is evil? #todo

## Matthew 5:43-47
Let us love our enemies.  
Enemies are also children of God.  
It changes nothing if you only love those who love you.  
Jesus' invitation is to live under another standard.  
Radical thinking like our heavenly Father.

# Matthew 6

## Matthew 6:1-6  
Let us not be righteous only to be seen: like those who do good things, but at the same time make a YouTube video to get more views. Or those who pray so others can see how great their ability to communicate with God is.

## Matthew 6:7  
Don't pray saying meaningless words.

## Matthew 6:9-13  
The Lord's Prayer. #todo

## Matthew 6:14-15  
Since our heavenly Father forgives us, we also have to forgive those who offend us.

## Matthew 6:16-18  
Don't fast to be seen, do it in secret. What is fasting? #todo

## Matthew 6:19-21  
Let us not worry about accumulating the things of this world, since they will perish. But let us accumulate heavenly riches. What are those heavenly riches? #todo 

## Matthew 6:22-23  
The eye is the lamp of the body. The eye receives what is outside.  
If our eye receives good things, our interior will be well.  
Otherwise, it will be bad.  
What does Jesus mean by "how great will be the darkness"? #todo

## Matthew 6:24  
We can only serve one master: God or money, pleasures, goals, etc.

## Matthew 6:25-34  
Let us not worry about anything, because God loves us so much that He gives us what we need.  
Instead of worrying, let us seek His Kingdom and His righteousness, and everything else will be added.
