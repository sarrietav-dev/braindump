The argument follows this logical progression:

1. Everything that has a beginning has a cause.
2. The universe has a beginning.
3. Therefore the universe has a cause.

The first premise is grounded in [[Everything that has a beginning has a cause|the law of causality]], a foundational principle in both science and philosophy.

The second premise is supported by [[SURGE — Five Lines of Evidence for the Big Bang|S.U.R.G.E.]], which presents scientific evidence for a universe with a definite beginning.

This version of the argument is often reffered as the **Kalam Cosmological Argument**
