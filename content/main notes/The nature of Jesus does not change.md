[[The heart of Jesus is only for the penitent|For His own]] His [[Jesus is gentle and humble of heart|gentle and humble]] character is eternal and unchanging. It does not depend on:
- Our sins.
- Weaknesses.
- Insecurities.
- Doubts
- Anxieties
- Failures.
