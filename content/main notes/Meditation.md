# What

Mediation means to regularly think deeply about something. In this case is to think deeply about the word of God.

# Why

This practice helps us to acquire more godly wisdom, faith and our soul rejoices. Also deepens our knowledge in his word.

# How

You might meditate by praying for understanding of the word and repeating it over and over, day and night.

Good passages to meditate:

- The 10 commandments
- The Lord's prayer
- The beatitudes
- Jesus' parables
- The psalms
- The epigrams of James.
