Confession is the act of telling God, in prayer, about the sin that you have done. This comes before [[Repentance]].

We have to confess our sins because God doesn't rejoice in the person who doesn't do that ([[probably because of pride]]). Also, we can find grace when we confess.

> [!quote]
> Whoever conceals their sins does not prosper, but the one who confesses and renounces them finds mercy.
>
> — Proverbs 28:13

To confess, first we have to recognize before God that we are sinners, and then we have to tell each sin specifically.
