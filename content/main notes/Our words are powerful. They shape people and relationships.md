Words can have a big effect on others. They can either build up or tear down.

For example:
- Gossiping can bring anxiety and a bad look for the other person.
- Shunning someone for their sins might keep them down.
- Flattering damages your integrity.

# Bible support

James 3:5-6 How great a forest is set ablaze by such a small fire! **6** And the tongue is a fire, a world of unrighteousness.
