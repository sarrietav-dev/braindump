The Big Bang theory posits that the universe began as an extremely hot, dense point approximately 13.8 billion years ago and has been expanding ever since. While we can't directly observe the event itself, multiple lines of evidence support its validity.

- S – [[The Second Law of Thermodynamics]]
- U – [[The Expansion of the Universe|Universe is expanding]]
- R – [[The Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB)|Radiation afterglow (Cosmic Microwave Background)]]
- G – [[The Galaxy Seeds in the CMB|Great Galaxy Seeds]]
- E – [[Einstein's General Theory of Relativity]]
