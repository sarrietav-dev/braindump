When in [[Jesus is gentle and humble of heart|Matthew 11:29]] Jesus says that He is humble in heart, He is not referring to the virtue of humility, but to a state of lowliness or destitution in life.

For example:

- “He has brought down rulers from their thrones; and has exalted the *humble*” — Luke 1:52
- “… Do not be proud in your thinking, but associate with the *humble*…” — Romans 12:16

This leads us to know that Jesus is an accessible person. For Him, there are no prerequisites or obstacles to come to Him.

All He needs is an open disposition from us.
