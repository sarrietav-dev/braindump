A model is a purposeful abstraction: a simplified representation of a domain or system designed to solve a specific problem. In Domain-Driven Design (DDD), a model expresses the domain logic and guides implementation.

Importantly, a model is **not a full map** of the domain—it omits irrelevant detail to stay useful. A model that tries to represent everything risks becoming as complex as the domain itself.

The usefulness of a model comes from its clarity and alignment with both the business needs and the software's purpose.
