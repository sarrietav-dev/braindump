Christian life is not an easy one. 

We have to fight the good battle of faith. That's the path to eternal salvation.

Like Jesus who went through many difficulties up to His crucifixion. The good fight is to follow Jesus and to keep His commandments until He comes back. 

Thankfully, we have our Lord who we look for strength and nourishment and the throes of battle, even when we fail. 

He guards us, preserves us and keeps up to the end.
